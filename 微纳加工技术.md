### 第二小节 光刻工艺详解

光刻过程中分为**八个**步骤

#### 1.硅片清洗（Wafer Cleaning）

![31f86a31ae4de2773b7d2613ff0b1c0f](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/31f86a31ae4de2773b7d2613ff0b1c0f.png)

清洗步骤包括：

- 化学清洗（Chemical clean）
- 去离子水漂净（Rinse）
- 甩干（Dry）

For removing **contaminants** and **particulate** and **improving photoresist adhesion**

#### 2.预烘及涂底胶（Pre-bake and Primer vapor coating）

![d36dc514e0e57450d41548784ff31b15](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/d36dc514e0e57450d41548784ff31b15.png)

![d831c1d1e89c8d07f2d20747f0c887fe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/d831c1d1e89c8d07f2d20747f0c887fe.png)

- 预烘烤（Pre-bake）

  目的：

  - **Dehydration** bake
  - Remove **moisture** from wafer surface
  - Promote adhesion between **PR** and **surface**（提升光刻精度）
  - Usually **around 100°C**
  - Integration with **primer coating**

- 涂底胶（Primer vapor coating）

  目的：

  - Promotes adhesion of **PR** to **wafer**（提升光刻精度）
  - widely used: **HMDS**（很薄，促进光刻胶和硅片表面的结合）
  - HMDS vapor coating **prior to PR coating**
  - Chill plate to **cool down wafer before PR coating**

#### 3.涂光刻胶（PR Coating）

![3aeeffb926bbce93a10b8dcd45a5b2b1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/3aeeffb926bbce93a10b8dcd45a5b2b1.png)

![1ee9e68ff6843625758058d798454ccb](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/1ee9e68ff6843625758058d798454ccb.png)

- Wafer sit on a vacuum chuck
- Rotate at high speed
- Liquid photoresist applied **at center of wafer**
- Photoresist spread **by centrifugal force**
- Evenly coat on wafer surface

光刻胶的旋涂厚度与**光刻胶种类**、**粘度**和**旋转速度**有关

**EBR（Edge bead removal）**是为了去除在旋涂过程中附着在wafer边缘的光刻胶，避免在后续过程中产生污染物颗粒

#### 4.软烤（Soft Bake）

![ad6d3ba4a986b746d02631f8b2520fce](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/ad6d3ba4a986b746d02631f8b2520fce.png)

![4eb020bac885d85325e0097c5250b38b](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/4eb020bac885d85325e0097c5250b38b.png)

- Evaporating most of solvent (> 80%) in PR
- Solvents help to **make a thin PR** but **absorb radiation** and **affect adhesion**
- Soft baking **time and temperature** are **determined by PR types** and **specific process**
- 90-110°C for 30min in oven; 10-15min for hotplate（**depends on specific process**）
- ==Over bake:== polymerized, less photo-sensitivity
- ==Under bake:== affect adhesion and exposure

#### 5.对准（Alignment）

![339196eac88c7213a7e26536056373f2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/339196eac88c7213a7e26536056373f2.png)

#### 6.曝光（Exposure）

![83a2ecc00534b235f2298aad44cc716c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/83a2ecc00534b235f2298aad44cc716c.png)

![e340836ed575e3de991e7797d79faf1a](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/e340836ed575e3de991e7797d79faf1a.png)

- **Most critical process** for IC fabrication
- Determines the **minimum feature size**
- Most popular used photolithography tool in the advanced IC fabs
- Use of **Deep UV light**

#### 7.后烤（Post exposure bake）

![e6bc25bca76fe87aec2e2d492d930fa5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/e6bc25bca76fe87aec2e2d492d930fa5.png)

#### 8.显影、硬烤及检测（Development、 Hard bake and PI）

![210880dceead2fc4a0ed9122b60b0bd1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/210880dceead2fc4a0ed9122b60b0bd1.png)

**显影：**

![c53dc2fa4fcecaa74a99548d99dcde05](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/c53dc2fa4fcecaa74a99548d99dcde05.png)

- Transfer the pattern from mask or reticle to photoresist
- Three basic steps:
  - Development
  - Rinse
  - Spin Dry

显影效果：

![8c637802b58269187be4c4e65f29ece6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/8c637802b58269187be4c4e65f29ece6.png)

**硬烤：**

- Evaporating all solvents in PR

- Improving **etch** and **implantation** resistance

- Improve PR adhesion with surface

- **Polymerize** and **stabilize** photoresist

- PR flow to **fill pinhole**

  ![7b790963c5dd79dd261027a153905933](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/you416/Markdown-online-note/group-meeting-note/7b790963c5dd79dd261027a153905933.png)

- **Hard bake temperature** normally is **higher than** the **soft bake temperature** for the same kind of photoresist

**检测（Inspection）：**

- Inspection, stripped PR and rework (==if in need==)
  - Photoresist pattern is temporary
  - Etch or ion implantation pattern is permanent

- Photolithography process can rework **but can not** after etch or implantation
- Optical microscope for large feature size and Scanning electron microscope (SEM) for smaller one (< 0.5um)
